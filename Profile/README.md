## 🎨 How to Use Axure RP 9 for Prototyping

### 1. Download and Install Axure RP 9
To download Axure RP 9, visit the official [Axure RP download page](https://www.axure.com/) and install it on your system.

### 2. Understanding the Interface
- **Workspace**: The workspace is where you create your wireframes and prototypes.
- **Widgets**: Use widgets such as buttons, text fields, and images to build your interface.
- **Interactions**: Link widgets together using interactions and transitions to make your prototype interactive.
- **Preview**: Once you’ve completed your prototype, use the Preview feature to view it in a browser.

### 3. Export and Share
- **Export**: Once you have finished designing your prototype, export it as an HTML file or share a link directly from Axure to showcase your work.

### 4. Submission
- Once you have completed the practical or assignment, you’ll be instructed on how to submit your files or provide a link to your prototype.
